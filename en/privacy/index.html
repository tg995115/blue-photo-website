6. Storage, international processing, and retention

ItemPolicy
LocationsThe Operator is based in Singapore. The main Blue Photo backend is hosted in Google Cloud's Seoul region. Apple, Google, OpenAI, and their service providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate
Protection during transferBlue Photo takes appropriate steps to ensure overseas recipients are bound by legally enforceable obligations to protect personal data to a standard at least comparable to the Singapore PDPA, unless an applicable exception applies
General retentionInformation is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Service, protect users, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations, and is then deleted or anonymised
Account deletionA deletion request starts a 30-day recovery period. After that period, Account information and Account activity are deleted when required external-provider and security steps are complete, except information that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, or backup-protection purposes
Deletion protection recordAfter permanent deletion is authorised, Blue Photo retains a non-reversible Account fingerprint and quarantined Identity Platform UID hash for 35 days so a database backup cannot restore a deleted Account
Device informationApp tokens, settings, and cached images may remain on the device until removed by the app, the user, Account deletion, or the operating system
Provider recordsApple, Google, AdMob, OpenAI, and other providers retain information according to their own policies
